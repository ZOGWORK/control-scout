Welcome to the 2026/2027 FIRST Tech Challenge New Jersey Competition Season!
We are immensely grateful to the FIRST Tech Challenge New Jersey Planning Committee, and to our 300+ volunteers for generously giving of their time and talents to make the FIRST Tech Challenge New Jersey Program possible!

​The FTCNJ Planning Committee extends our sincere gratitude to our NJ Sponsors:  PSEG Foundation and Wipro Foundation

​​Event Registration:​
  1. For full details on registering to compete in New Jersey, please visit the "Event Registration and Acceptance Criteria" page ​
  2. ​​All Event Rankings can be found at:     ​ftc-events.firstinspires.org
  3. ​Teams must be fully registered and paid with FIRST and FTCNJ in order to compete.
    1. There is a $100 Season Fee, payable to FIRST Tech Challenge New Jersey, that must be received before a team can attend any competition event in New Jersey.
      1. ​We highly recommend that you process the FTCNJ payment at the same time you process the FIRST payment.
      2. Please fully fill out and use This Invoice​ to submit FTCNJ Season Fee.
        Please note: Coaches are responsible for getting this Invoice to their purchasing organizations as required.​
​Important Registration Note:
The preferred capacity for NJ Meets is 24 teams, while we have increased the capacity at some events to meet this year's growth, if there are declines from within the Preferred registration slots, we will lower capacity back to 24 teams before inciting from the waitlist.
Please check the "Important Season Dates" page!
